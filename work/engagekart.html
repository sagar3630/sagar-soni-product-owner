The product

Businesses connect their WhatsApp Business number. Teams share one inbox, send broadcast campaigns to labelled audiences, set keyword-based smart replies, and route conversations to the right agent during business hours.

Web app with shared inbox, broadcasts, smart replies and automation.

What I did

  • Wrote the product documentationBRDs, functional specs and user stories for the inbox, broadcasts, smart replies and automation.
  • Ran competitor analysisAgainst established WhatsApp tools, to find gaps and decide which features actually mattered to clients.
  • Kept delivery on the roadmapWorked with stakeholders sprint by sprint in an Agile setup.
  • Designed screens in FigmaFrom wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, to get stakeholders aligned before development.

Calls I made

Lead with the shared inbox, not just bulk sending

Context

Many competitors pitched mass messaging first.

Decision

Put the team inbox, with labels, assignment and smart replies, at the centre of the product, and treat broadcasts as one way in.

Why

Conversations are where leads convert and customers get helped. Broadcasts alone risk spam and account restrictions.

Outcome

Inbox first

a feature set refined through competitor research, which improved client satisfaction with the product.
