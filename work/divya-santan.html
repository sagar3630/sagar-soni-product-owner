The product

Parents browse consultants by category, book and pay for sessions, then consult over chat and video and get advice and prescriptions. Consultants manage availability and earnings, and admins approve consultants and control pricing, content and notifications. The client brought a prototype, and the job was to turn it into a buildable production MVP.

One app for parents and consultants, plus an admin portal.

What I did

  • Ran discovery and MVP scopingWorked with the foundation to separate what launch needs from what can wait, and wrote it into a scope document the client signed off.
  • Wrote the backlogOne story per user flow, with screens, success flow, acceptance criteria, edge cases and notifications, loaded into Jira under module epics.
  • Built high-fidelity flowsAI-assisted mockups for every surface: booking, cancel and reschedule, changing consultant mid-plan, and consultant payouts.
  • Mapped every notificationBy event and channel (push, SMS and email) within each channel's limits, including India's DLT rules for SMS.
  • Settled the money rulesConsultant fee versus display price, discounts, GST, tax deducted at source, and payouts through a payment gateway.

Calls I made

Consultants set their own fee, the platform sets the price

Context

The prototype had no clear line between what a consultant earns and what a parent pays.

Decision

Consultants enter their fee at onboarding. Admin sets the display price, the fee plus the platform margin, during review. The fee is locked once approved.

Why

Consultants see one predictable number, and the business controls margin and discounts in one place.

Let parents change consultant partway through a plan

Context

Care plans run over many sessions, and the fit with a consultant doesn't always work.

Decision

Allow one change per part of the plan while at least one session is still unbooked, and handle any fee difference through admin.

Why

Parents keep their plan instead of abandoning it, and the rule stays simple enough to build and support.

Bring self-serve reschedule into the MVP

Context

Rescheduling was first parked as out of scope.

Decision

Bring it into the MVP for both parents and consultants, with clear notice windows.

Why

Without it, every change in a pregnancy schedule becomes a support ticket.
