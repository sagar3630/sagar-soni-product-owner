Project overview

Parents browse consultants by category, book and pay for sessions, then consult over chat and video and get advice and prescriptions. Consultants manage availability and earnings, and admins approve consultants and control pricing, content and notifications. The client brought a prototype, and the job was to turn it into a buildable production MVP.

One app for parents and consultants, plus an admin portal.

Calls I made

Consultants set their fee, we set the price

Context
The prototype didn't separate what a consultant earns from what a parent pays.
Decision
Consultants enter their fee when they join. Admin adds the platform's share to set the price parents see. The fee is fixed once approved.
Why
Consultants always know what they'll earn, and the business manages margins and discounts in one place.

Let parents switch consultants mid-plan

Context
Care plans run over many sessions, and sometimes a consultant isn't the right fit.
Decision
Parents can switch once per part of a plan, as long as a session is still unbooked. Admin sorts out any price difference.
Why
Parents keep their plan instead of quitting, and the rule is simple to build and support.

Let people reschedule on their own from day one

Context
Rescheduling was first left out of the first version.
Decision
Add it for both parents and consultants, with clear rules on how much notice is needed.
Why
Pregnancy plans change often. Without this, every change becomes a support call.

Suggest an answer to every open question

Context
46 questions were waiting on the client, and any one of them could hold up the work.
Decision
Send them all as one list, each with our suggested answer, and build with that answer unless the client says no.
Why
The team keeps building, and the client only has to reply where they disagree.

Write the spec from what was actually built

Context
After weeks of building, the product no longer matched the original stories or the prototype.
Decision
Write the spec from the real code, and copy each feature's details into its Jira ticket.
Why
Testers check what's really there, and the business approves the real product.
