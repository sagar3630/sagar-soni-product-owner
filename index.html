Decisions

Calls I made

16 product decisions from five products. Each one links to its context, the decision and why.

  • Love Ya Catering Count money from our own records, not Google DecisionUse Google Analytics only to count website visits. Take order and sales numbers from our own database. Context and why
  • Love Ya Catering Let each restaurant set its own time and distance limits DecisionEach restaurant sets how long it has to accept an order and how far it delivers. Admin can change it if needed. Context and why
  • Love Ya Catering Fix what blocks the launch first DecisionPut it all in one list, write each item clearly, and rank it: fix before launch, fix next sprint, or fix later. Context and why
  • Love Ya Catering Track 53 things at launch, not 129 DecisionLaunch with the 53 events that answer real questions. Move 55 to later and drop 21 we didn't need. Context and why
  • Thousand Greens Show new members their chances before they ask DecisionShow each club as a High, Medium or Low chance of a yes, and point new members to High and Medium clubs until they've played a few games. Context and why
  • Thousand Greens Compare with last year, not last month DecisionCheck the full history first. The dip happened every winter in colder places, and overall the number was growing. Context and why
  • Thousand Greens Check each feature does its job, not just that it works DecisionIn every review, check what changed, the rule behind it, and whether the result is really happening for users. Context and why
  • Thousand Greens Give referral credit however the friend joins DecisionMatch the friend's email when they sign up, and give back the credit members had already missed. Context and why
  • Divya Santan Consultants set their fee, we set the price DecisionConsultants enter their fee when they join. Admin adds the platform's share to set the price parents see. The fee is fixed once approved. Context and why
  • Divya Santan Let parents switch consultants mid-plan DecisionParents can switch once per part of a plan, as long as a session is still unbooked. Admin sorts out any price difference. Context and why
  • Divya Santan Let people reschedule on their own from day one DecisionAdd it for both parents and consultants, with clear rules on how much notice is needed. Context and why
  • Divya Santan Suggest an answer to every open question DecisionSend them all as one list, each with our suggested answer, and build with that answer unless the client says no. Context and why
  • Divya Santan Write the spec from what was actually built DecisionWrite the spec from the real code, and copy each feature's details into its Jira ticket. Context and why
  • TaxVero Turn every user email into a ticket DecisionLog each one as a ticket with the steps to repeat it and how urgent it is, and follow it until it's fixed. Context and why
  • TaxVero List the tricky cases up front DecisionEvery story lists its tricky cases as part of its checks, so testers and developers agree on what done means. Context and why
  • EngageKart Build around the shared inbox, not mass messages DecisionMake the shared team inbox, with labels, assigning and quick replies, the heart of the product. Mass messages are just one feature. Context and why
